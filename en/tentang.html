About the author

Dr. Despinur Dara, S.T., M.M. is both an academic and a practitioner, with more than twenty years of her career devoted to human resource management. As a permanent lecturer at the Faculty of Economics, Universitas Negeri Jakarta, she is known for deep expertise in strategic HRM, training and development, and talent management.

Her credentials are reinforced by professional certification as a Certified Professional in Human Capital Management and a Certified Human Capital Business Partner. She favours an innovative approach that brings digital technology and quantitative methods into both teaching and HR practice, which allows her to bridge theory and practice.

As an active researcher, she pays close attention to digital transformation in work and organizations. Her articles appear in reputable Scopus-indexed international journals on themes such as job crafting, employee well-being, job satisfaction, and technology in well-being. Through this work she is committed to developing a future workforce that is adaptive, creative, and capable of meeting the demands of a digital working era.

About this showcase

Every paper here follows the same structure: background, research gap, problem statement, novelty, theoretical landscape, method, findings, contribution, and then the part almost nobody publishes, namely the review journey with the reviewer comments round by round and how they were answered.

Manuscripts still under review are also shown, with their titles and journals deliberately withheld to respect the blind review process. Once accepted, the page opens up in full. Readers can therefore follow the entire life cycle of a piece of scholarship as it really happens, rather than seeing only the polished final result.

The site is updated whenever something changes: a new paper appears, a manuscript moves through the pipeline, or another set of reviewer comments is summarised. If you are wrestling with your own first publication, these pages are meant to keep you company through it.
