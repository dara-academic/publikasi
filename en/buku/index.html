Newest Diramedia Kreasindo · April 2026

Human at Work: Wellbeing dan Produktivitas Karyawan di Era Digital

Dr. Despinur Dara and Dr. Donny Maha Putra · Editor: Prof. Dewi Susita

Companies talk about employee well-being more than ever, with meditation apps, yoga sessions, and happiness surveys. Yet more people come home from work with a tiredness that sleep does not fix. This book offers an honest answer: workers are constantly asked to be tougher and better at managing their time, when the real source of the problem is often how the work is designed, how leaders lead, and how technology is used at work. The examples come from Indonesian working life, from civil servants and lecturers to small business owners and ride-hailing drivers.

Skill-Up! Strategi Bertahan dan Unggul di Dunia Kerja Digital

Dr. Despinur Dara

How to survive, grow, and excel in the middle of rapid digital transformation. Working life now demands a high capacity to adapt, not only technically but also in mindset and social skill. The book sets out concrete strategies grounded in research and global trends, from strengthening digital literacy and soft skills to building a professional portfolio on digital platforms. Every chapter carries critical reflection, real case studies, and exercises that can be applied straight away.

Published 2025Category Reference bookISBN 978-634-7174-38-3
Diramedia Kreasindo

Design Thinking dalam Manajemen SDM

Dr. Despinur Dara and Dr. Donny Maha Putra

Bringing design thinking into human resource management. Rather than drafting policy at the management desk and hoping staff will adjust, this approach starts from the real needs of the people who will live with it, then designs, tests, and refines in cycles.
