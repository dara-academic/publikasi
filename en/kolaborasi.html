Why collaborations are shown separately

In the Scopus and SINTA clusters, Dr. Dara is the first author, which is why each work is taken apart at length, from the research gap through to the reviewer comments. Her role here is different: a supporting author who helps students and colleagues bring their own work into print.

The presentation is therefore different too. What you see is not a dissection but a concise catalogue that respects the intellectual ownership of the lead authors, with direct links so readers go to the original source. Most of the articles here are a first publication for the student who wrote them, and that is the most valuable part of this list.
