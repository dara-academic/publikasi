Hybrid work in the public sector: practical insights on organizational support

Despinur Dara (first author), Naura Yasmin Zahraini, and Resi Rejalia, Faculty of Economics and Business, Universitas Negeri Jakarta.

Qualitative research article · Open access · DOI: 10.1108/JWAM-07-2025-0129 · Journal page

In brief

Most hybrid work studies busy themselves measuring productivity. This one takes a different route, exploring how Indonesian civil servants interpret and construct the meaning of hybrid work, with attention to its symbolic, intersubjective, and contextual dimensions. The result shows that organizational support is not merely formal policy but a meaning built collectively through institutional rhythms and everyday expectations.

A useful lesson: a qualitative study with ten informants can reach a Q1 journal, provided the interpretive depth and theoretical positioning are strong. Sample size is not everything.

This is also the fastest acceptance in this showcase, at roughly five weeks. The title itself changed during review, from an earlier version, at the reviewers suggestion.
