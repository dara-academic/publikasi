Burnout latency in AI-driven work systems: modelling the silent interval between technostress and burnout

Despinur Dara (first author, corresponding author) and Dewi Susita, Faculty of Economics and Business, Universitas Negeri Jakarta.

Vol. 34 No. 8 (2026) · Conceptual paper · DOI: 10.1108/IJOA-12-2025-6316 · Journal page

In brief

This paper introduces a new concept called burnout latency: the quiet stage in AI-supported work where people still deliver their usual output while their focus, energy, and emotional capacity are steadily draining away. It answers a puzzle organizations increasingly face, namely why complaints of burnout keep rising even when productivity figures look stable.

In one line: burnout is not a sudden event but the end point of a long process, and most of that process is invisible to output-based monitoring.

The review journey behind this paper is the most dramatic in this showcase. One reviewer recommended outright rejection, calling the argument lost in the complexity of its own language. The editor treated that recommendation as a major revision instead, and after three rounds the paper was accepted.
