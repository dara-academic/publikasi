Exploring the dynamics of work satisfaction and innovation among young lecturers in post-pandemic Indonesia

Despinur Dara (first author, corresponding author) and Saparuddin, Faculty of Economics and Business, Universitas Negeri Jakarta.

Vol. 40 No. 1 (2025), pp. 17-43 · Open access · DOI: 10.22146/jieb.v40i1.9972 · Journal page

In brief

      
This study investigates the relationships between work autonomy, work crafting, job satisfaction, and innovative work behaviour among young Indonesian lecturers after the pandemic. Its most striking result is a rejected hypothesis: work autonomy did not raise job satisfaction. In fact the coefficient was negative. Excessive autonomy without institutional support actually lowers satisfaction.
The message for universities: granting freedom is not enough on its own. Young lecturers need a balance between autonomy and genuine institutional support.
This paper also holds the longest review journey in this showcase, with five review rounds spanning January to July 2024 before publication in 2025.
