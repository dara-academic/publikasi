Effectiveness analysis of Nadine implementation at Indonesia's MoF: a combined approach using importance-performance analysis and causal relationship analysis

Despinur Dara (first author, Universitas Negeri Jakarta) and Donny Maha Putra (corresponding author, Universitas Pembangunan Nasional Veteran Jakarta).

Vol. 10 No. 1 (2025), pp. 53-67 · Open access, CC BY-SA 4.0 · Journal page

In brief

      
This paper evaluates the effectiveness of Nadine, the digital official correspondence system used across Indonesia’s Ministry of Finance. Drawing on responses from more than 2,500 staff, it measures user satisfaction, identifies priorities for improvement, and tests the relationship between policy success and application performance.
The practical insight: policy success and application performance are closely linked. Improving the software is not merely a technical exercise, it lifts the effectiveness of the policy behind it.
Grounded in innovation diffusion theory and the unified theory of acceptance and use of technology, the study contributes real-world evidence from a developing-country public administration, a context still underrepresented in the literature.
