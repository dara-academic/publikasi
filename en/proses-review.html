Six manuscripts are being assessed at international journals such as Elsevier, Wiley, Springer, Emerald, and Sage. Their status is shown exactly as it stands, so the length of the road to publication is visible.

While a manuscript is still being assessed, its title and journal are deliberately withheld. That is the rule of blind review, where authors and reviewers are not supposed to know each other. Once a paper is accepted, its identity is revealed and its card moves to In Production.
